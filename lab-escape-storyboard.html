Lab C: Bond energy challenge

N≡N (945) + O=O (498) → 2 N=O (630 each). Work out ΔH for the reaction.

Think like an examiner
  • Polystyrene cup + lid: poor conductor, so less heat is lost to the surroundings.
  • Energy level diagram: label reactants and products, draw ΔH as a single-headed arrow. Salt A warms the water, so its products sit lower.
  • The reverse reaction has the same size of ΔH with the opposite sign: −183 kJ/mol.

Think like an examiner
  • Small chips: the curve is steeper at the start but levels off at the same final volume (same mass of CaCO₃).

Lab I: Dynamic, not dead

Think like an examiner
  • Heating NH₄Cl: white solid disappears at the bottom, then white solid reappears higher up the cooler glass as NH₃ and HCl recombine. NH₄Cl(s) ⇌ NH₃(g) + HCl(g)
  • Position of equilibrium: name the side with fewer gas molecules (pressure) or the endothermic direction (temperature).
  • A catalyst speeds up both directions equally, so the position does not move.
